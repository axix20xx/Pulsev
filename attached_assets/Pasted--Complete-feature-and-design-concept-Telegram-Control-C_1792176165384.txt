## Complete feature and design concept: Telegram Control Center

### 1. Product structure
Use **one centrally managed Telegram bot** for the system. The super admin controls its token and global behavior; resellers and customers link their own Telegram accounts and receive only the messages their role permits.

Build two connected parts:

1. **A Bot Center inside the admin panel** for configuration, recipients, notification rules, and delivery monitoring.
2. **A role-aware Telegram experience** with a different main keyboard and permitted information for super admins, resellers, and customers.

---

## 2. Bot Center UI in the panel

### Main page: overview
A polished, responsive **Telegram Control Center** page with:

- **Top status bar:** Connected / Needs attention, bot name, last successful message, and a test-message button.
- **Summary cards:** linked recipients, messages sent today, delivery success rate, and messages needing attention.
- **Live activity feed:** recent account, sale, service, provider, and delivery events.
- **Delivery health panel:** queued, sent, retried, and failed messages.
- **Quick actions:** Test bot, pause sending, open notification rules, and view delivery log.

On desktop, use a dashboard grid with a compact sidebar or tab navigation. On phones, stack the cards, keep the primary action easy to reach, and display delivery records as cards instead of a cramped table.

### Suggested sections
- **Overview**
- **Activity & audit**
- **Recipients**
- **Notification rules**
- **Message templates**
- **Delivery history**
- **Bot settings**

### Bot settings
- Save, test, replace, pause, or remove the Telegram bot token.
- Show a masked token after saving; never show it in full again.
- Display the bot’s name, connection state, and last error.
- Set default language, timezone, and quiet hours.
- Choose which global alerts go to super admins.
- Add an admin test recipient.

### Recipients
A searchable list of linked accounts with:

- Name, panel role, Telegram connection state, and last message time.
- Filter by role, active connection, and delivery problem.
- Revoke or reset a link.
- See notification categories enabled by the recipient.

A reseller must never see another reseller’s recipients.

### Notification rules
A visual rule builder could let admins select:

- **Event:** for example, service deleted.
- **Recipients:** super admins, a specific admin group, or the record’s reseller.
- **Priority:** normal, important, or urgent.
- **Delivery:** immediately or in a digest.
- **Message details:** safe fields to include and a link to the panel page.

### Message templates
Preview and edit the wording of alerts in Persian and English. Include placeholders such as event type, actor, affected record, time, and a panel link. Prevent sensitive fields—passwords, tokens, VPN configurations, private subscription links—from being used in templates.

## 3. Events and logs

The bot should send **meaningful event alerts**, not every raw application or server log line.

### Super-admin event feed
Allow global notifications for:

- **Users and resellers:** created, edited, disabled, reactivated, or deleted.
- **Sales:** created, edited, paid, refunded, or removed from the sales log.
- **Services:** created, renewed, edited, activated, disabled, cancelled, or deleted.
- **Requests:** submitted, approved, rejected, or failed.
- **Provider operations:** success, timeout, failure, or partial completion.
- **Usage and workers:** polling stopped, polling delayed, quota reached, or repeated errors.
- **Accounting:** payment recorded, balance changed, or payment request received.
- **Security and delivery:** unusual activity, bot errors, or repeated Telegram delivery failure.

For important events, show **who did it, what happened, which record was affected, when it happened, and the reason if recorded**. Include a link to the appropriate panel page.

### Reseller event feed
Resellers receive only events related to their own customers, sales, and services:

- Customer or service created, edited, disabled, reactivated, or deleted.
- Sale recorded, paid, edited, refunded, or removed from their sales log.
- Service renewed, expired soon, reached a usage threshold, or failed an operation.
- Customer request or storefront inquiry received.
- Their payment or account balance changed.

### Optional customer messages
Customers may opt in to receive:

- Service creation or renewal confirmation.
- Expiry reminders.
- Usage warning or quota reached.
- Service status changes.

Customer messages should not include reseller accounting, internal logs, provider details, or staff activity.

### Audit and deletion handling
Keep a panel audit record for every create, edit, and delete event. A deletion alert should preserve the actor, affected record reference, time, and reason—even if the original record is removed. The Telegram message should link back to the panel; deletion and other risky actions should still require confirmation inside the authenticated panel.

## 4. Telegram’s main keyboard

Use a persistent, role-specific keyboard. Keep buttons short and organized into two columns; use inline buttons beneath individual messages for context-specific actions.

### Super-admin keyboard
```text
📊 داشبورد        🕒 رویدادها
👥 کاربران        🧾 فروش‌ها
🛰 سرویس‌ها       ⚠️ وضعیت سیستم
🔔 اعلان‌ها       ⚙️ تنظیمات
```

Possible command behavior:

- **Dashboard:** overall business and operations summary.
- **Events:** recent user, sale, and service activity.
- **Users / Sales / Services:** role-authorized summaries with links to panel pages.
- **System status:** provider and worker health, recent failures.
- **Notifications:** manage categories and quiet hours.
- **Settings:** help, account connection, and notification preferences.

### Reseller keyboard
```text
📊 خلاصه امروز    👥 مشتریان
🧾 فروش‌ها        🛰 سرویس‌ها
⏳ نزدیک انقضا    🔔 اعلان‌ها
⚙️ تنظیمات
```

Every result must be limited to that reseller’s own business data.

### Customer keyboard
```text
🔐 سرویس من       📅 تاریخ انقضا
📈 میزان مصرف     🔔 اعلان‌ها
💬 پشتیبانی
```

Only show service details after confirming the Telegram account is linked to the customer’s panel account.

### Inline buttons on messages
For a service or sales alert, buttons could include:

- **Open in panel**
- **View details**
- **Mute this alert type**
- **Mark as reviewed** — for authorized staff only

Avoid one-tap deletion, renewal, or provider-changing actions in the initial release.

## 5. Example alert layout

A Persian notification should be compact and easy to scan:

```text
🧾 فروش جدید ثبت شد

مشتری: [نام مشتری]
فروشنده: [نام فروشنده]
مبلغ: [مبلغ]
ثبت‌کننده: [کاربر یا مدیر]
زمان: [زمان محلی]

[مشاهده در پنل] [اعلان‌های فروش]
```

For a deletion:

```text
🗑 رکورد فروش حذف شد

حذف‌کننده: [نام و نقش]
رکورد: [شناسه یا شرح کوتاه]
دلیل: [در صورت ثبت]
زمان: [زمان محلی]

[مشاهده گزارش رویداد]
```

Only include safe record details—no passwords, access tokens, VPN configuration, or private subscription URLs.

---

## 6. Commands and interaction
Start with safe, mostly read-only commands:

- `/start` — link the account or show help.
- `/today` — summary appropriate to the user’s role.
- `/events` — recent events the user is allowed to see.
- `/expiring` — services nearing expiry, within role scope.
- `/alerts` — recent alerts.
- `/settings` — notification preferences.
- `/help` — command guide.

Use panel links for detailed views and any sensitive or destructive action.

---

## 7. Delivery, preferences, and privacy
- Let each recipient choose alert categories, immediate messages versus digests, quiet hours, timezone, and language.
- Provide **Pause notifications** and **Disconnect Telegram**.
- Prevent duplicate alerts for the same event.
- Retry temporary delivery failures a limited number of times.
- Record delivery state: queued, sent, failed, or retried.
- Show persistent failures to the super admin.
- Keep global admin logs separate from reseller and customer notifications.
- Redact secrets and sensitive personal data from messages and delivery records.

---

## 8. Recommended release order

**First release:** Bot Center overview and token setup, connection test, secure account linking, role-based keyboards, super-admin notifications for user/sale/service create-edit-delete events, reseller-scoped sale/service notifications, and delivery history.

**Next:** provider and worker alerts, usage and expiry thresholds, quiet hours, message templates, and daily digests.

**Later:** optional customer notifications, richer command-based summaries, and additional safe workflow shortcuts.
